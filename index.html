Game options

    What should the CSV look like?

    One question per row, with a header row on top:

    question,option1,option2,option3,option4,answer
                                  "What is 2 + 2?",3,4,5,6,2
                                  "Capital of Japan?",Osaka,Kyoto,Tokyo,Nagoya,Tokyo
    • Header names are flexible. Question, Option A, choice_1, Correct Answer and similar all work.
    • The answer column can be a number (1–4), a letter (A–D), or the full text of the correct option.
    • Commas and line breaks inside a field are fine as long as the field is wrapped in double quotes.
    • Comma, semicolon and tab separated files are all detected automatically.
    • Blank rows are skipped. Rows with problems are reported with their line number.
